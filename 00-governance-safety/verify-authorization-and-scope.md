# Purpose
Determine whether security work is authorized and establish clear scope.

# Required Inputs
- Asset owner or authorized organization
- Written authorization, owned-lab/CTF status, or public program scope
- Approved targets, methods, testing window, and prohibited actions

# Engagement Gate
If ownership, authorization, or scope is unclear, do not provide operational guidance that enables unauthorized access, exploitation, credential theft, persistence, evasion, or disruption. Request clarification or provide defensive design and remediation guidance only.

# Required Output
1. Authorization and scope status
2. Allowed objectives
3. Explicit out-of-scope boundaries
4. Safe methodology and non-disruption constraints
5. Evidence handling and reporting plan
